Our Story

Founded in 2019 by Kenny Sizemore and Mark Cook, Hop House Tennessee Taps is more than just a bar – it's a celebration of Tennessee's craft beer scene.

We focus on Tennessee-brewed beers, featuring 30 rotating taps from local breweries and 6 regional selections. Our goal is to create a relaxing atmosphere that brings our community together.
